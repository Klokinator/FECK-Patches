ExtendWeaponDescBox by Vesly (FE8), ported to FE7U by FECK. The R button's help box grows from 3 rows to
5, so a weapon's description gets 3 lines. Text rows 4 and 5 use two more text handles after the game's three
(HelpTextHandles+0x38 and +0x40) and the OBJ tiles after the box's (+0x1800 to +0x27FF from its VRAM).

Unlike the FE8 patch, rows 4 and 5 are only taken when a box needs them and they fit in OBJ VRAM: a box of 3 rows
or fewer leaves the tiles after it alone, and a taller box with no room is cut to 3 rows with its extra lines
dropped, as the unpatched game does. The game's addresses come from the game's .inc file.

The stat screen loads the help box at the default 0x6013000 too, but it draws its page title, page arrows,
page number and portrait frame with the OBJ tiles after that box (0x6014800 on), so rows 4 and 5 there drew over
the page title. StatScreenHelpGfx (hooked in the stat screen's help setup) loads its box at 0x6011000 instead:
tiles 0x80 to 0x1BF with rows 4 and 5, which no page of the stat screen uses (the map's unit sprites are there,
and the stat screen hides the map). Checked in BizHawk 2.11.1 on all three pages, and that the map's sprites
are whole after the stat screen closes.
